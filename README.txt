NEON CYBER PLATFORMER — COMPLETE UPGRADE

FEATURES
1. Ten uniquely designed platform levels.
2. Difficulty increases as you progress, with more hazards and faster movement.
3. Moving drone enemies and spike hazards.
4. Level 10 final boss with a health bar and shield/core phases. Jump onto its exposed core three times to defeat it. Collect all Level 10 orbs and reach the exit to see the final victory screen.

ALSO INCLUDED
- Mobile touch controls for left, right, and jump.
- On-screen NEXT LEVEL / PLAY AGAIN action on the completion screen; Enter also works on a keyboard.
- Start menu, instructions, pause/resume, restart level, sound effects, particles, lives, score, and locally saved personal best.
- Touch input is released safely if a touch is interrupted or the tab loses focus.

CONTROLS
Desktop: A/D or Left/Right arrows to move; W, Up arrow, or Space to jump; P or Escape to pause; Enter to continue after a level.
Mobile: Use the on-screen left, right, JUMP, and pause buttons. Tap NEXT LEVEL on the completion screen.

UPDATE YOUR EXISTING GITHUB PAGES SITE
1. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
2. Open your GitHub repository: neon-cyber-platformer.
3. Choose Add file > Upload files.
4. Upload and replace these five files from this folder: index.html, style.css, game.js, audio.js, particles.js.
5. Scroll down. Use the commit message: Upgrade Neon Cyber Platformer with 10 levels and boss.
6. Click Commit changes.
7. In Settings > Pages, keep Deploy from a branch, branch main, folder /(root).
8. Wait for GitHub Pages to finish deploying. Open your published game and hard-refresh (Ctrl+F5 on desktop). On a phone, reopen the site in a private/incognito tab if the old version is cached.

NOTES
- Upload all five files together so the HTML, CSS, game logic, audio, and particles stay in sync.
- Personal best is saved only in the current browser/device; it is not an online leaderboard.
